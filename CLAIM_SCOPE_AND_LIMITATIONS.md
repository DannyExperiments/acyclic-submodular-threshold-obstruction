# Claim scope and limitations

The explicit nine-member family satisfies the one-corner condition and has an acyclic full dependency digraph in the Boolean lattice on six ground elements and in its generated eighteen-element sublattice. No real-valued submodular function induces it as a strict sublevel set in either ambient lattice.

The paper also verifies a separate adaptation of Simmons, Bansal and Cheriyan's published construction. The six-element witness uses the ordinary meet and join inequalities directly. Priority and minimality remain open. The equivalent separation-system formulation follows from Elbracht, Kneip and Teegen's existing reduction.

The appendix applies classical linear inequality duality to finite lattices. The paper proves its claims through explicit finite data and a symbolic contradiction. The replay verifies the data. No proof-assistant formalization is included.
